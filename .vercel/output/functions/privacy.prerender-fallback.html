Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

TikTok Shop Guard is operated by Metamorphosis LLC. This policy explains what information we collect when you use tiktokshopguard.com and how we use it.

What We Collect

Account information: Your name, email address, and hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.

TikTok connection data: OAuth tokens, TikTok Shop ID, seller ID, and shop name when you connect your shop. We do not store your TikTok password.

Shop and listing data: Product listings and metadata fetched from the TikTok API to perform your requested scans. Not shared with third parties.

Violation and appeal data: Scan results, flagged violations, and appeal drafts tied to your account and accessible only by you.

Payment information: Processed by Stripe. We never see or store your credit card details.

How We Use AI

When you use the Appeal Drafter, your listing description and violation type are sent to Anthropic to generate a draft email. We do not use your content to train AI models or for advertising.

Service Providers

We share data only with: Supabase (database and auth), TikTok Open Platform (shop data via OAuth), Anthropic (AI for appeal drafting), Stripe (billing), and Vercel (hosting).

Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. To request deletion email support@tiktokshopguard.com and we will process it within 30 days. OAuth tokens are immediately invalidated when you disconnect your shop or delete your account.

Security

We use TLS encryption, hashed passwords, and row-level security. OAuth tokens are stored server-side and never exposed to the client browser.

Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your information through your account settings or by contacting us. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information.
